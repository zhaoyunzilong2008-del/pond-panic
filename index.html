POND SURVIVED

What happened? Every fisher had a reason to catch fish before somebody else did, but everyone shared the cost of a depleted pond. As the fish became scarce, their price rose, which made catching the remaining fish even more tempting.

That is the economic tension in the game: an individually rewarding choice can create a worse shared outcome when access to a resource is open and nobody owns the long-term stock.
